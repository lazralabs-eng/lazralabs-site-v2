IV   Governance

Discretion, engineered.

A store's numbers are not for everyone. Nexus is built so the right people see the right figures, and nothing changes without a trace.

Access by role and store

Owner, general manager, manager, technician. Each sign-in sees its own stores and its own view. Nothing more.

Every change recorded

Who moved it, when, and from where. History is kept, never overwritten.

Private by default

Every dashboard sits behind your own sign-in. There is no public door.

Yours to leave

No DMS integration required. No lock-in. Your data stays your own.

Behind the surface, a small private roster of agents does some of the reading. They are introduced in person.
